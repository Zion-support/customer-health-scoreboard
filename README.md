## What it does

Customer Health Scoreboard unifies usage, support, billing, and relationship signals into one health score per account — and fires the right playbook automatically when health moves.

## Key features

- **Unified health score** — usage + tickets + NPS + billing in one number.
- **Playbook triggers** — score drops auto-launch CSM tasks and sequences.
- **Portfolio heatmap** — health across your book by segment and CSM.
- **Expansion radar** — healthy accounts with growth signals flagged for upsell.
- **Exec digest** — weekly health movement narrated for leadership.

## Who it's for

CS teams managing more accounts than they can personally watch.
